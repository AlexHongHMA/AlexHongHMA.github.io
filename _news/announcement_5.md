Won the **Best Presentation Award** at the 16th Instrumentation, Control and Automation Senior Project Conference (ICA-SP Con 2025) for **"Natural Gas Methane Leak Detection Using an Infrared Camera"** :microphone:

This was my senior project at KMUTT, supervised by Asst. Prof. Dr. Sarawan Wongsa. It became the foundation for the 3D-CNN work that later won Best Paper at [IEEE ICoICT 2025]({{ '/news/announcement_3/' | relative_url }}).
