Our paper **"Development of high-speed inspection technique based on motion-induced eddy current testing for surface railway defects"** has been published in _Nondestructive Testing and Evaluation_ (Taylor & Francis), a journal ranked **Quartile 1 (Q1) on Web of Science**.

The work was carried out at the **Maintenance Technology Center (MTC), KMUTT**, under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Bovornchok Poopat, Asst. Prof. Dr. Chetsadaporn Janya-anurak, Asst. Prof. Dr. Cherdpong Jomdecha, Asst. Prof. Chaloemkiat Jirarunsatian, and Lt. Raksit Mutthanu.

> Brienza, N., N. Khunsombutcharoen, B. Poopat, C. Janya-anurak, C. Jirarunsatian, R. Mutthanu, H. M. Aung, and C. Jomdecha. 2026. "Development of High-Speed Inspection Technique Based on Motion-Induced Eddy Current Testing for Surface Railway Defects." _Nondestructive Testing and Evaluation_, March, 1–22. [doi:10.1080/10589759.2026.2638558](https://doi.org/10.1080/10589759.2026.2638558)
