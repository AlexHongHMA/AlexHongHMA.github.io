## Starting a robotics lab in Yangon

In 2019 I founded the **Laboratory of Robotics and Artificial Intelligence (L.O.R.A)** because the equipment I wanted to learn on did not exist anywhere I could reach. Neither did the people, at first. Over the next two years it became a place where students could take apart a problem without needing permission.

When COVID-19 reached Myanmar, we shifted to building autonomous disinfection robots. That project taught me more about talking to public-health officials and sourcing parts under sanctions than it did about control theory, which was not the lesson I expected.

## Mentoring at FIRST GLOBAL

I mentored Myanmar's national team at the **FIRST GLOBAL Challenge 2019** in Dubai. We took home the Judges Award. What I remember is not the trophy but the week beforehand, watching fifteen-year-olds argue about drivetrain geometry at midnight and being entirely certain they were right.

Teaching is still the part of engineering I find hardest to give up. If you are a student in Myanmar or Thailand working on something in robotics or machine learning, my inbox is open.
