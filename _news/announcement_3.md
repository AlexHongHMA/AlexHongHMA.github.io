Our paper **"Optimised 3D-CNN for Real-Time Infrared Natural Gas Leak Classification: Balancing Accuracy and Computational Cost"** won the **Best Paper Award** at the 13th International Conference on Information and Communication Technology (ICoICT 2025) in Bandung, Indonesia :indonesia: :trophy:

The work was done with Asst. Prof. Dr. Sarawan Wongsa at KMUTT. It presents a 3D convolutional network that reaches **99.75% accuracy** classifying methane leaks in infrared video, optimised through input-resolution scaling and quantization so it can run on edge hardware.
